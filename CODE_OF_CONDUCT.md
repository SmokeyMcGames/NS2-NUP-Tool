# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, patient, and constructive. Discuss ideas and behavior without
personal attacks. Welcome people with different experience levels and backgrounds.
Respect privacy and keep discussion relevant to the project.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexualized conduct, spam, and
publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not use
project spaces to distribute unauthorized game content or credentials.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or limit
participation. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior, from a reminder
or warning to temporary or permanent removal for serious or repeated misconduct.
These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces
officially maintained for this project.

For conduct concerns, use GitHub's reporting controls or contact
[@SmokeyMcGames](https://github.com/SmokeyMcGames) through the public contact options
on their profile. Do not post sensitive details in a public issue. Maintainers will
handle available reports with discretion; no confidentiality or response-time
guarantee is implied.
